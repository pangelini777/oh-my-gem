Oh My Gem is a Gemini CLI port of Oh My OpenAgent / Oh My OpenCode.

This project is substantially based on, adapted from, and inspired by:

- Oh My OpenAgent / Oh My OpenCode
- Original author: YeonGyu Kim / code-yeongyu
- Repository: https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent
